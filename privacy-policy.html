1. Who we are

Sillycon is an independent mobile game studio based in Pakistan. We develop and publish free-to-play casual and hypercasual games for Android, built with the Unity engine and distributed through Google Play.

For the purposes of data protection laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Sillycon is the controller of the personal data processed by our games, except where a partner (such as an advertising network) acts as an independent controller as described below.

Contact: sillycon1998@gmail.com

2. Scope

This policy applies to all games and apps published by Sillycon on Google Play (the "Games"), to this website, and to any support communication with us. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we link to, which have their own privacy policies.

4. Information we do not collect

Unless a specific Game clearly says otherwise and asks for your permission, we do not collect your name, postal address, phone number, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or payment card details. We do not sell your personal information for money.

5. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Games, including saving progress and delivering purchased items.
  • To show advertisements, measure ad performance and limit how often you see the same ad.
  • To personalize ads where you have allowed it (see Consent and privacy choices).
  • To understand how players use the Games and improve gameplay, difficulty, content and performance.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes, bugs and technical issues.
  • To process and validate in-app purchases and to prevent fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To respond to support requests, feedback and privacy requests.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

6. Advertising

Our Games are free to play and are supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to show ads such as banners, interstitials and optional rewarded videos (watching a rewarded video is always your choice and earns an in-game reward).

To show and measure ads, AdMob and the ad networks it works with may automatically collect device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device information and information about your interactions with ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

  • Personalized ads are selected using information such as your Advertising ID and your past activity across apps, so they are more likely to match your interests.
  • Non-personalized ads are not based on your past behavior. They use only contextual information such as the app, your approximate (for example city-level) location and the time of day. Non-personalized ads still use identifiers and cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

Where the law requires consent, we only show personalized ads if you agree. You can always choose non-personalized ads.

Ad-free option

Some Games may offer an in-app purchase that removes forced ads (such as banners and interstitials). Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch may still be available after such a purchase.

These networks may receive information directly from your device, including your Advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad-interaction data, and they process it under their own privacy policies, often as independent controllers.

8. Consent and privacy choices

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, our Games show a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), which is certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. You can accept, reject or customize the use of your data for personalized ads, ad measurement and other purposes. If you do not consent, you will see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings or privacy options menu (where available), or by clearing the Game's data or reinstalling it, which shows the message again.

US state privacy messages

If you are in a US state with a comprehensive privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia or others), our Games may show a US state regulations message through Google's UMP that lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information and of targeted advertising. See also US state privacy rights.

Resetting or opting out of the Advertising ID

You control your Android Advertising ID from your device settings. On most Android devices go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. When the Advertising ID is deleted, apps receive a string of zeros instead of an identifier and ads cannot be personalized with it. Menu names can differ by device and Android version. Learn more in Google's Advertising ID documentation.

Other choices

  • You can turn off internet access to stop most data collection; some Games can be played offline.
  • You can uninstall a Game at any time to stop all further collection by that Game.
  • You can ask us to delete your data (see Data deletion).

9. In-app purchases

Some Games offer optional in-app purchases, for example consumable items (such as coins or extra lives that are used up), non-consumable items (such as permanently unlocked characters or levels) and ad-removal purchases.

  • All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, integrated through Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP). Your payment is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.
  • We never receive or store your payment card or bank details.
  • We receive transaction information such as the product purchased, price, currency, time, Google Play order ID, purchase token and receipt. We use it to deliver and restore your items, validate that purchases are genuine, prevent fraud, handle refunds and keep financial records required by law.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies. You can request one through Google Play (see Google Play refund help). If a refund is granted, the related item may be removed.
  • Parental controls: parents and guardians can require authentication for purchases in the Google Play Store settings and manage purchases with Google Family Link (see Google Play parental controls and Family Link).

10. Analytics and crash reporting

We use analytics and crash-reporting tools to understand how our Games are played and to fix problems. These tools collect usage data, device information, app instance or installation identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address and diagnostic data. Our Games may use:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: gameplay events, sessions, retention and engagement statistics. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Firebase Analytics policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and a Crashlytics installation ID. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Unity Gaming Services Analytics (and Unity engine diagnostics): gameplay events, device and performance information. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

We use analytics in aggregated form to make decisions about our Games. We do not use analytics data to identify you personally. Where consent is required, analytics that are not strictly necessary are only used in line with your choices.

11. Remote Config and A/B tests

Some Games use Firebase Remote Config, Firebase A/B Testing or Unity Remote Config to change settings (such as difficulty, ad frequency or rewards) without an app update and to test different versions of features with groups of players. These tools use installation identifiers and analytics data to assign you to a test group and measure results. They do not collect additional personal information.

12. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: personalized advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device where consent is required (for example under the ePrivacy Directive). You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing done before withdrawal.
  • Performance of a contract: providing the Game you chose to install and delivering items you buy.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalized ads to fund free Games, security and fraud prevention, crash reporting, aggregated analytics and improving our Games. We balance these interests against your rights, and you can object at any time.
  • Legal obligation: keeping transaction records, responding to lawful requests.

13. How we share information

  • Service providers who host, analyze, process or support our Games on our behalf (such as Google/Firebase and Unity), under contractual obligations.
  • Advertising partners listed in section 7, who collect information directly through their SDKs to show and measure ads.
  • Google Play, to process purchases and distribute our Games.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, court order or government request, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Sillycon, our players or others.
  • Business transfers: if Sillycon is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, allowing ad partners to collect identifiers for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing"; see US state privacy rights to opt out.

14. International transfers

Sillycon is based in Pakistan and our partners operate servers around the world, including in the United States. Your information may therefore be processed outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the partner's participation in recognized frameworks such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

15. Data retention

  • User-level analytics data is kept for no more than 14 months in Google Analytics for Firebase and for a similar limited period with other analytics providers; aggregated reports that do not identify you may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for about 90 days.
  • Purchase and transaction records may be kept for as long as required by tax and accounting laws.
  • Support emails are kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and for up to 2 years afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Advertising partners retain data according to their own privacy policies.

When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

16. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for data sent by our Games and reputable providers with strong security practices. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

17. Children's privacy

Our Games are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If a Sillycon Game is part of Google Play's Families program or targets children in its audience settings, that Game:

  • uses only ad SDKs from Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK program;
  • does not show personalized or interest-based ads, and requests ads as child-directed (for example using AdMob's "tag for child-directed treatment");
  • does not transmit the Android Advertising ID or other persistent identifiers from children for advertising purposes; and
  • complies with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us at sillycon1998@gmail.com. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

18. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy (data portability);
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your information;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent;
  • Not be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing that significantly affect you;
  • Complain to your local data protection authority (for example in the EU, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Swiss FDPIC).

Brazil (LGPD): users in Brazil have the rights listed in Article 18 of the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, including confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing, and withdrawal of consent. You may also complain to the ANPD.

How to exercise your rights: email sillycon1998@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request", say which Game and which right you want to exercise, and include your Android Advertising ID or a Google Play order ID (GPA.xxxx) so we can find your data. Because we do not use accounts, we may need these identifiers to match your request. We will respond within 30 days (or sooner where the law requires). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. For data held directly by our ad partners, you can also contact them using the links in section 7.

19. US state privacy rights (including California CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to request deletion and correction; to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising; to limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect sensitive personal information); and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

In the past 12 months we have collected the following categories of personal information: identifiers (such as Advertising ID and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay and ad interactions), geolocation data (approximate, from IP address), commercial information (purchase history) and inferences used for ads. These come from you and your device, and are used and disclosed for the purposes described in this policy.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: we do not sell personal information for money. However, letting ad partners collect identifiers to show targeted ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under these laws. To opt out you can: (1) use the US privacy option in the Game's settings where available; (2) delete or reset your Advertising ID as described in section 8; or (3) email us with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share". We also honor applicable opt-out preference signals where we can technically receive them. You may use an authorized agent to make a request; we may need to verify your request and the agent's authority.

20. Data deletion

You can ask us to delete data associated with your use of our Games at any time. Full instructions are on our Data Deletion page. Uninstalling a Game stops further collection, and resetting or deleting your Advertising ID breaks the link between past advertising data and your device.
