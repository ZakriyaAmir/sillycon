Data Deletion

Applies to all Sillycon games on Google Play · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Our games do not require an account, so there is no account to delete. You can still ask us to delete data associated with your use of any Sillycon game. This page explains how.

1. Send us a deletion request

Email sillycon1998@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • the name of the game(s);
  • your Android Advertising ID (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads; the ID is shown there on most devices) and/or
  • a Google Play order ID (starts with "GPA.") for any in-game purchase, found in your Google Play purchase history or receipt email;
  • any player ID shown in the game's settings, if the game shows one.

Because we don't use accounts, these identifiers are how we find your data. We may ask a follow-up question to confirm the request is genuine.

2. What we delete

  • Analytics and gameplay data linked to your identifiers in our analytics tools (Firebase / Google Analytics, Unity Analytics), where the tool supports user-level deletion;
  • Crash reports linked to your installation, where they can be identified;
  • Any player or progress data we store on servers for that game (game data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall);
  • Your support emails and contact details, once your request is complete.

3. What we may keep

  • Purchase and transaction records (order ID, item, amount, date) that we must keep for tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or legal reasons, for as long as the law requires;
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that no longer identify you;
  • A minimal record that we received and completed your request.

Data held by advertising partners is controlled by those partners. We will forward deletion requests where the partner supports it, and you can also contact them directly using the links in our Privacy Policy.

4. Timeline

We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days. Some backups may take up to an additional 30 days to be overwritten. Deleted purchases may not be restorable afterwards.

5. Things you can do right now

  • Uninstall the game: this stops all further data collection by that game and removes game data stored on your device.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads): this unlinks previously collected advertising data from your device.
  • Change your consent choices from the privacy options in the game, where available.

Questions? Email sillycon1998@gmail.com.
