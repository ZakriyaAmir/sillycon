Tall Crawl

You're a spider climbing an endless tree, and the only goal is to go higher than ever before. Dodge bees and wasps, squirrels, snakes and the bats that come out at night, as the tree cycles between day and night.

  • Boost through hurdles and enemies to knock them away and grow stronger.
  • Raise a shield, slow time with a freeze power, and swing on your web to escape tight spots.
  • Revive to keep climbing and beat your best height.
  • Spend coins in the shop to upgrade your powers and unlock 24 spider skins.
  • Simple one-thumb controls, short sessions, and a fresh challenge every climb.

Meet the developer

The person behind Sillycon.

Zakriya Amir

Founder & Lead Unity Developer

I'm a Lahore-based game developer with 9+ years of experience, and the founder of Sillycon. I currently also work as a Technical Project Manager at Userwise (since November 2025), running delivery for mobile games published with partners like HalfBrick and PeopleFun — including Fruit Ninja 2 and Jetpack Joyride, as well as Fruit Ninja VR, which shipped on Meta Quest. Before that I was Lead Unity Developer / Team Lead at Userwise (March 2024–November 2025).

My path runs from AR mobile games at AA'LA Solution, through 3D gameplay at Geniteam, AR/VR and hyper-casual titles at Invozone and AAA-style cross-platform architecture at Narsun Studios, to leading and mentoring 20+ Unity engineers at Userwise.
