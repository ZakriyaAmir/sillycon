Tall Crawl

You're a spider climbing an endless tree, and the only goal is to go higher than ever before. Dodge bees and wasps, squirrels, snakes and the bats that come out at night, as the tree cycles between day and night.

  • Boost through hurdles and enemies to knock them away and grow stronger.
  • Raise a shield, slow time with a freeze power, and swing on your web to escape tight spots.
  • Revive to keep climbing and beat your best height.
  • Spend coins in the shop to upgrade your powers and unlock 24 spider skins.
  • Simple one-thumb controls, short sessions, and a fresh challenge every climb.
