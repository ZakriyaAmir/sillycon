Support

We're a small studio and we read every message.

Need help?

Bug reports, missing purchases, feedback or business enquiries.

Email sillycon1998@gmail.com

To help us help you faster, please include the game name, your device model, your Android version, the game version (shown on the Google Play page or in the game's settings) and, for purchases, the Google Play order ID (GPA.xxxx). Screenshots are welcome.

FAQ

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Restart the game while connected to the internet; pending purchases are usually delivered on the next launch. Non-consumable items (such as "Remove ads") can be restored by reinstalling the game with the same Google account. If it still doesn't work, email us with your order ID.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Google Play, so refunds are handled by Google under its refund policies. See Google Play refund help.

Can I play without ads?

Some games offer an optional purchase to remove forced ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.

How do I stop personalized ads?

Use the privacy options in the game (where available) or delete/reset your Advertising ID in your Android settings. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

See our Data Deletion page.

How do I stop my child making purchases?

Turn on purchase authentication in the Google Play Store settings or use Google Family Link. See Google Play parental controls.
